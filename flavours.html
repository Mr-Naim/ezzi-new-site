Applications

A single flavour rarely stays in one lane — the same base note is re-tuned differently for a hot beverage than for a chewy sweet.

Tea & Coffee

Black, green, rooibos and herbal infusions; hazelnut-mocha to fruit notes for cappuccino powders, ground coffee and coffee syrups.

Beverages

Concentrated liquid flavours for soft drinks and spray-dried versions for instant beverages — citrus, cola and fruit, tested for solubility and stability.

Bakery & Confectionery

Hard caramels, chewing gum, chocolates, cookies and pastries, with natural flavour components and vanilla extracts.

Dairy

Concentrated fruit preparations for yoghurt, sweet notes for milk beverages, and fruity flavour for ice cream.

Cereal

Natural cream, cocoa, banana and cranberry notes for muesli bars and crispy cereals, liquid or powder.

Oral Care & Animal Feed

Mint-oil-based toothpaste and mouthwash flavourings; liquid, powder and spray-dried flavours for feed mixes.

Formats & Technology

The same flavour profile behaves differently depending on the format it's delivered in — so we build it in the one the application actually needs.

Liquid Flavours

Concentrated liquid formats for teas, beverages, fruit gums, chewing gums, soft and chewy candies, chocolates, fruit preparations, ice cream and oral care.

Powder & Granules

Spray-dried powders, macro-granules and plant-fibre granules for cereal, bakery, ice cream, dessert powders and instant beverages.

Encapsulated Granules

Granulated flavour in a water-soluble matrix, engineered for mechanical stability, low hygroscopy and a long shelf life — built for tea and instant-beverage formats, in a range of shapes and sizes.

Mint Specialisation

Gently rectified mint oils — peppermint, spearmint and menthol — for oral care, gum and confectionery.

Vanilla Portfolio

Vanillin through to certified-organic vanilla extract, produced from whole vanilla pods.

Development & Production

Our flavour team tracks global taste trends and shapes them into profiles for tea, coffee, beverages, confectionery, bakery, oral care and animal feed. Every flavour goes through application testing in real product matrices — baked goods, confections, beverages — before it's signed off. Production runs from small trial quantities up to 25-tonne batches, under HACCP- and IFS-monitored conditions, with warehouse capacity built for short turnaround.

Organic & Certified Options

For brands building toward clean-label and sustainability claims, we also supply organic and Fairtrade-certified flavours meeting EU Regulation 2018/848 — alongside all-natural, non-GMO, gluten-free and allergen-free variants where the application calls for it.
