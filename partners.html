Our Partners

The Manufacturers Behind Every Batch

EZZI Perfumery House brings together principal manufacturers from Singapore, Germany and India — each a specialist in their own category — so every flavour, fragrance, colour and spice we supply carries the quality of the people who actually make it.

Meet Them

Our Principal Partners

SENSES International

Singapore

A Singapore-based fragrance house established in 2009, building customised olfactory solutions across air care, home care, personal care, fine fragrance, industrial, natural and allergen-free lines — sourcing essential oils direct from farm distillation for its own exclusive blends.

Supplies our Fragrances range

FREY & LAU

Germany

A mid-sized manufacturer based north of Hamburg with over 180 years of experience refining essential oils, flavours, fragrances and aroma chemicals — serving beverage, food, cosmetic, fabric care, home care and pharmaceutical industries under a GMP-certified, REACH-compliant quality system.

Supplies our Fragrances range Supplies our Flavours range

Aryan Chemicals

India

Operating from Surat, Gujarat since 2009, Aryan Chemicals manufactures synthetic and natural food colours, pharma colours, and cosmetic pigments — each shade shipped with its CI number, E-number or FD&C/D&C designation for regulatory sign-off, and exported to customers worldwide.

Supplies our Colours range

AVT McCormick

India

A 30-year joint venture between the A.V. Thomas Group (India) and McCormick & Company (USA), sustainably sourcing and steam-sterilising 20+ spices — pepper, cumin, turmeric, ginger, cinnamon and more — alongside custom seasoning blends built to a customer's own specification.

Supplies our Seasoning range
