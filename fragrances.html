Laundry & Fabric Softener

Natural & Allergen-Free

Two dedicated lines cover the categories that need the most careful sourcing.

Natural Fragrance

Built from essential oils and botanical extracts, sustainably sourced, for aromatherapy, natural toiletries and natural laundry lines.

Allergen-Free Fragrance

Formulated without the allergens listed under the EU cosmetics directive — for infant products and sensitive-skin lines.

Food-Contact Grade

Fragrance for products that share space with food and beverage production — lip care, food-service cleaning agents, sanitising wipes and hand soaps — held to food-safety-grade standards.

Fragrance Families

Every composition we build sits somewhere on this map of families — useful shorthand when briefing a new scent.

Citrus

Lemon, bergamot, mandarin and grapefruit — sparkling and fresh.

Fruity

Apple, cherry, banana, mango and pineapple, often paired with floral or musk.

Floral

Rose, lily of the valley and jasmine — sensual, from fresh-floral to sweet-floral.

Woody

Sandalwood, cedar, patchouli and vetiver — dried or freshly cut wood, bark and roots.

Balsamic-Sweet

Vanillin, ethylvanillin, heliotropin and cumarin — warm and sensual.

Oriental

White blossom, clove, cardamom, pepper, patchouli and oud — sensual-spicy.

Floriental

The lightness of floral notes with the opulence of oriental accents.

Herbal-Leathery

Lavender, rosemary, mugwort and sage — fresh, clear and natural.

Development & Quality

Every scent moves through the same path: concept and presentation, composition, then stability and compliance testing — colour trueness, physical stability and conformity with IFRA guidelines — before it reaches production. Batch sizes run from small trial quantities up to multi-tonne runs, on just-in-time schedules or annual contracts, so a fragrance that works in the lab still performs a year and a warehouse later.
