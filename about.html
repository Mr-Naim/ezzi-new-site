We Are EZZI Perfumery House

The journey of EZZI Perfumery House began in 1978 as a small family business dealing in perfumes, food colours, food chemicals and essences — based in Mitford, near Bhandari Medicine Market, Dhaka.

Today, with over 48 years of industry experience, we serve domestic and international industries, households, restaurants and lounges with a variety of flavours, essences, oils, perfumes, beverages and food products, delivered with customized service and the best quality.

Customer satisfaction and serving healthy, quality products remain our utmost priority, as we work toward becoming one of the leading companies by international standards. In quality, we never compromise.
